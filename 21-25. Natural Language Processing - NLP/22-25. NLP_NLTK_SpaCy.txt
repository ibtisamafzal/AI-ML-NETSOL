Lecture 24:


Lemmatization and stemming are two popular text‑preprocessing techniques in NLP used to reduce words to their base form.
stemming cuts words down to their root by trimming endings,
lemmatization uses linguistic rules to return meaningful base words (lemmas).


Stemming: Finds the base word from similar words

- from nltk.stem import PorterStemmer
- Stemming don't always give you correct output
- Uses of stemming: Search Engines, Text Classification, Information Retrieval, Document Matching

Example Output:
play -> play
playing -> play
played -> play
plays -> play



Lemmatization:
Useful where stemming doesn't work. Used to understand the meaning.
