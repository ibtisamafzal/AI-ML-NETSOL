What is Vectorization?
Vectorization is the process of converting non-numerical data (such as text, images, or raw features) into these lists of numbers (vectors) so a Machine Learning model can process them.


While vectorization is the broad umbrella concept of turning data into numbers, embeddings are a specific, intelligent type of vectorization.

Think of it this way: All embeddings are vectors, but not all vectors are embeddings.


Embeddings:
Embeddings are a specific technique of vectorization that captures semantic meaning, context, and relationships in a dense format.
