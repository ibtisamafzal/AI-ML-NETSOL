Lecture-10:

Measures of Dispersion (How spread out is the data?) -> Variance, Standard Deviation, IQR

Variance (Data Analysis) vs Anomaly (Data Cleaning)

Standard Deviation -> Important for feature scaling -> Logistic/Linear Regression, SVM, KNN, PCS, K-Means

Coefficient of Variation (CV): Measures the size of standard deviation relative to the mean -> Uses: Quality Control, Sensor Data

Skewness -> Right Skewed, Left Skewed, Negative Skewness (Uses: Statistical Assumptions, Visualization, Outlier Detection, Transformations)

Kurtosis -> Describes the tail behavior of skewness (Types: Mesokurtic (Normal), Leptokurtic (Heavier Tails), Platykurtic (Lighter Tails))

Z-Score -> How many standard deviations an observation is away from the mean.

Percentile -> Relative position of an obserbation compared with the rest of the data.

Quartiles -> Divide ordered data in four parts.

Interquartile Range (IQR) -> Difference bw two quartiles.
