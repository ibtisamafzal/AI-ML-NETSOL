Choosing the Right Degree:


Underfitting (Too Low):
- Fails to capture the true non-linear patterns in the data
- leading to High Error


Overfitting (Too High):
- Makes the model overly sensitive
- Complex Architecture on simple data
- Causes it to memorize random noise
- oscillate wildly between data points


Best Practice:
- Use cross-validation
- use separate validation dataset to test different degrees
- Pick the one which gives lowest validation error
